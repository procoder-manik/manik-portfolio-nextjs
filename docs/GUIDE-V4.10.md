# Update guide: version 4.10 (AI everywhere, more FAQs, Help center)

## Write with AI: where to find it

| Where | What it writes |
| --- | --- |
| **Pages > Add page** or any page > **Write with AI** (top right) | A whole page from your own section types (hero, cards, steps, pricing, FAQ, call to action...) plus its SEO. Pick the kind of page, say what it is about, optionally a keyword and real details. Choose "Replace the sections" or "Add after the current ones". |
| **Pages > a built-in page** (Home, Portfolio, Blog...) > **Write with AI** | The headings and intros of the page's built-in parts plus its SEO. Projects, posts and prices still come from their own screens. |
| **Case studies > Add new** (opens by itself) or **Write with AI** | Headline, summary, overview, challenges, approach, results, tags and SEO from a short brief. Pick a portfolio project to reuse its details. |
| **Portfolio > Add new** | Unchanged from 4.8: the project writer and banner prompts. |
| **The sparkle button on every section** (Sections tab, any page or case study) | The texts of that one section, written for the page it sits on. Works on built-in parts that have a heading and intro too. |
| **The small "AI" button next to a field label** | That one field. Type an optional instruction, then **Write**, **Improve** or **Shorter**. It appears on text, long text and Markdown fields in every editor and settings screen (pages, posts, projects, case studies, taught answers, services, packages, products, Home page texts...). Not on links, prices, names, dates or code. |
| **SEO tab > Write all SEO with AI** | Focus keyword, related keywords, meta title (with your brand), meta description, social title and description, breadcrumb title and schema in one click. "Only empty fields" keeps what you wrote. |
| **Social tab** and **Schema tab** | Their own one-click buttons: social texts, and the best schema type with its fields. |
| **Meta title, Meta description, Social title, Social description, Focus keyword** | Each has its own AI button for a new version of just that field. |

These buttons also work on the built-in page SEO screens (Admin > SEO > a built-in page).

## How the AI keeps to your site

Every request includes your name and role, your services with their real prices, recent projects, the pages that exist (the only links it may use) and your meta title format. It writes in the first person, never invents numbers, results, quotes, prices or dates, removes long dashes and stock AI phrases, and only keeps numbers you typed yourself (case study results, page notes). Schema never gets prices, ratings or dates. Nothing is saved until you press Save or Create.

It uses the same free AI keys as the AI writer (Admin > AI writer > Settings). When the free AI is busy it waits about 20 seconds and tries once more.

- 30 new answers were written: website design (copy, stores, landing pages, what to send, revisions), SEO (keyword research, technical fixes), automation and CRM, maintenance, payments and contracts, hosting, domains and email, security and testing, and a new **Help and support** topic.

- **/support**, **/help-center**, **/helpcenter** and **/knowledge-base** now open the Help center.
- Edit, hide or add answers in **Admin > Taught answers**; the Answer field has its own AI button.
