# Update guide: version 4.9 (built-in pages in Pages, menus on every page)

## What changed

Every page the site was built with now appears in **Admin > Pages**, at the top of the list and marked **Built-in**:

Home, Portfolio, Case studies, Blog, Resume, Pricing, Contact, Products, FAQ, Help center.

Your own pages are listed under them, marked "Your page". They are created automatically the first time you open Pages after updating.

## Editing a built-in page

Open it from **Pages**. The tabs are the same as for your own pages.

- **Page tab**
  - **Title**: the name in the list and the default text of its menu links.
  - **Published**: off hides the page (visitors get "page not found") and removes its links from the header, footer and sitemap. Turn it on again any time; nothing is lost. Home is always on.
  - **Menus**: see below.
- **Sections tab**: the page's own parts are shown as sections marked **Built-in**.
  - Move them with the arrows, hide them with the eye, or remove them with the bin.
  - Click a part to change its heading and intro (empty uses the built-in text shown in grey).
  - **Add section** adds any ready-made section (hero, text, cards, form, FAQ, pricing...) anywhere between the parts.
  - A removed part can be added back from **Add section > Built-in parts of this page you removed**.
- **SEO, Social, Schema, Advanced, Code**: the same as for your own pages. They save to the same place as the older "Page SEO" screens, so both always show the same values.

A built-in page cannot be deleted, copied or moved to a new address. Switch Published off instead.

The texts of the parts (projects, posts, packages, resume, home texts...) are still edited where they were. Each part has a link to its screen.

**Home:** the order of home sections, and which ones show, is now set in **Pages > Home > Sections**. The "Visible sections" switches in Home page texts were replaced by a link to it; your old choices were copied over.

## Menus

Every page (built-in and your own) has a **Menus** box:

- **Show in the header menu**, with its link text.
- **Show in the footer**, with its link text and the footer column it goes in.

Saving the page writes the link into **Header and footer**, so there is still one list of links. Change the order of links there.

To take **Case studies** out of the menu: Pages > Case studies > Menus, switch both off, Save. The old "Add Case studies to the menu automatically" switch was removed; the link it used to add is now a normal link you control.

## Quick test

- [ ] Pages: ten Built-in pages at the top, your pages below.
- [ ] Case studies > Menus: switch header and footer off, Save. The link is gone from the site.
- [ ] Portfolio > Sections: open Page heading, type a new heading, add a Text section between the heading and the grid, Save. View the page.
- [ ] Pricing > Published off, Save: /pricing shows "not found" and Pricing disappears from the menus. Switch it on again.
- [ ] Home > Sections: hide About, move FAQ up, Save. Check the home page.
- [ ] Your own page > Menus > Show in the header menu, Save: the link appears in the header.
