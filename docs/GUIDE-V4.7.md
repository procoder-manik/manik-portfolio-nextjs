# Update guide: version 4.7 (floating buttons)

## Back to Top: on or off, left or right

**Admin > Site > Site and contact > Features**

- **Back to Top button**: switch it on or off for the whole site.
- **Back to Top side**: Right or Left (desktop and phone).

## Chat: on or off, left or right

**Admin > Site > Analytics and integrations > Chat**

- **Chat on the site**: Built-in assistant, Third-party, or Off (this was already there).
- **Chat button side**: Left or Right (shown when the built-in assistant is chosen).

## Defaults

The chat is now on the **left** and Back to Top on the **right**, so they no longer crowd each other. This applies to your live site too, because the new option has a default and your saved settings simply gain it.

If you choose the same side for both, Back to Top automatically moves above the chat button so they do not overlap.

A third-party chat (Tawk, Crisp, Tidio) places itself; choose its side inside that tool.

## Quick test

- [ ] Open the site: chat on the left, Back to Top (after scrolling) on the right.
- [ ] Features: turn Back to Top off, save, reload: the button is gone. Turn it on again.
- [ ] Chat settings: set the side to Right, save: chat moves right and Back to Top is above it.
- [ ] Set Back to Top to Left: both on the left, Back to Top above the chat.
- [ ] Open the chat window on each side: it opens above the button and fits on a phone.
- [ ] Chat mode Off: no chat button; Back to Top drops to the corner.
